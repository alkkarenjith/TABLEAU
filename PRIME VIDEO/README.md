Exploring the Content Landscape of Amazon Prime Video Through Data Visualization, this project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze the platform’s extensive collection of movies and TV shows. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of key metrics, including total titles, total movies, total TV shows, content distribution by country, genres, release years, and content type.
The dashboard uses multiple visualizations to explore the Prime Video catalog, including genre popularity, shows by release year and type, content distribution by country, movies vs. TV shows, top categories, and title duration. Interactive filters are incorporated to allow users to explore the content based on different categories and duration ranges.
A key aspect of this project is the combination of content-type analysis, geographical distribution, genre trends, release-year analysis, and duration analysis within a single interactive dashboard, providing a clear view of the composition and evolution of the Prime Video content library. 
This project demonstrates proficiency in Tableau, data visualization, data analysis, interactive dashboard development, KPI design, filtering, and extracting meaningful insights from entertainment datasets.
